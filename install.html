Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Duo Checker

Changed a setting? Drag it again and delete the old bookmark.

  1. Open apple.com/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-duo in a tab you'll leave open.
  2. Click Duo Checker in your bookmarks bar. A panel appears in the bottom-right corner.
  3. Press Test notification and check that it reaches your phone.
  4. Leave the tab open (it can be in the background). Clicking the bookmark again hides or shows the panel.
